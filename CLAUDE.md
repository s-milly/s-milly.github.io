# Notes for Claude

Static GitHub Pages site (no build step). Design brief and decisions: `docs/BRIEF.md`.

- `index.html`: markup. Sections in order: pinned scroll scene (#about: intro, student, baseball, golf), #experience timeline, #projects grid, #skills (heading on the left on wide screens, résumé skills in three plain columns, a logo and a name per line; logos from Simple Icons, CC0; drawn line icons for SQL, LightGBM, NLTK, Power BI and Excel, which have no free logo), #contact.
- `site.js`: scene engine (scroll progress lerped; `measureCard` places the baseball card, which turns over once on the student beat and stays on its back; the back's text fades and the photo window grows out of the card's outline at the merge into a rounded frame, `END` in site.js, so the dark page stays around the baseball and golf photos; on desktop the golf plate is sized to that frame so the whole green and the ball show), suit-to-uniform merge, nav underline for the section in view, email copy button, timeline gold line fill (the jobs themselves are always shown) and details toggles, GitHub repo loader, and the signature "pitch" (clicking a project throws a baseball at the card, then opens it).
- `mascots.js`: the two scene mascots, built in JS. The ball is `position: fixed` on body so it never leaves the screen; the bat, burst and golf flag live in the stage (the flag inside `.plate--golf`). The ball sits on the card, pushes it over, points at the student line and is pitched into the baseball photo, all scrubbed by scroll; the bat's hit and the ball's flight back onto the end of the baseball heading's first line play once on a clock (WAAPI), then the bat leaves. By scroll again, the ball walks to the cup in the golf photo and tends the flag, then after the scene leaps onto the experience timeline and hangs from the tip of the gold line (`mascotWorld()` in site.js gives the tip). Scrolling back above the card merge resets the hit. Reduced motion: still poses that fade, hanging from the bottom of the full line.
- `site.css`: tokens on `:root` (dark, Clarion gold accent). Phone layout under `max-width: 760px`.
- `projects.js`: user-editable config (`githubUser`, `featured`, `hide`, `projects`).

Rules kept so far: no em dashes in visible copy, no invented stats, only numbers from Shawn's résumé. The field photo must stay painted (opacity ≥ 0.02) behind the headshot; hiding it breaks iOS. Verify desktop (1440x900), phone (390x844) and reduced-motion after scroll changes.
